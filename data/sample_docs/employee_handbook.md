# Employee Handbook

Helios Analytics publishes this handbook for every employee. It describes time off, remote work, leave, and expenses. Managers apply these rules consistently across teams.

## Working time

The standard week is 40 hours, Monday through Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Employees record exceptions in the HR portal.

## Paid vacation

Employees receive 28 paid vacation days per year. Submit vacation requests in the HR portal at least 10 business days in advance. Unused vacation may carry over up to 5 days into the next calendar year. The carry-over balance expires on 31 March.

## Remote work

Remote work is limited to 3 days per week. The other days are spent in an assigned Helios office. Remote days must be in a country where Helios already employs people. Employees keep their company laptop patched and use the corporate VPN on untrusted networks.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave can start up to two weeks before the expected date of birth or placement. Employees tell People Operations at least 30 days before the leave starts when the date is known.

## Expenses

Employees submit expense reports within 30 days of the cost. Travel, client meals, and approved home-office equipment are reimbursable when a receipt is attached. People Operations rejects reports that miss a receipt or a business purpose.

## Conduct

Employees treat colleagues and customers with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are grounds for disciplinary action. Concerns go to the manager or to People Operations when the manager is involved.
