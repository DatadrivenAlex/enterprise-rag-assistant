# Data Retention Policy

Helios Analytics keeps data only for as long as a stated business or legal need exists. This policy lists the default retention periods for the main company datasets. Privacy reviews exceptions.

## Customer analytics

Customer analytics records are retained for 24 months, then deleted or anonymized. The period starts on the date the event was collected. Aggregated statistics that cannot be tied back to a customer may be kept longer.

## Support tickets

Support tickets are retained for 36 months after the ticket is closed. Attachments follow the same period unless a legal hold is in place. Agents do not copy ticket exports into personal drives.

## Access logs

Production access logs are retained for 12 months. The security team may export a narrower slice during an active investigation. That export is deleted when the investigation closes.

## Employee records

Employee records are retained for the duration of employment and for 6 years after the person leaves. Payroll records follow the same 6 year period. People Operations owns deletion once the period ends.

## Privacy requests

Customers and employees send privacy requests to privacy@helios.example. Helios responds within 30 days. A legal hold pauses deletion for the records named in the hold and for no other records.
