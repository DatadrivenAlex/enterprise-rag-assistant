# Information Security Policy

This policy sets the baseline security duties for Helios Analytics employees and contractors. It protects customer data, internal analytics, and production systems.

## Accounts and access

Multi-factor authentication is required for every production system and for company email. Access follows least privilege. Managers review access when someone changes role and remove it on the last day of employment. Shared passwords are not allowed.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and the managed endpoint agent. Employees report a lost or stolen laptop to the service desk immediately. Personal storage of customer extracts is prohibited.

## Security incidents

Report security incidents to security@helios.example within 1 hour of discovery. An incident includes a suspected account compromise, a lost laptop with company data, exposed credentials, or an unexpected production change. Employees preserve evidence and wait for the security team. Employees do not attempt to investigate malware themselves.

## Acceptable use

Company systems are for company work. Employees do not send confidential datasets to personal email or to unsanctioned chat tools. Security awareness training is completed once a year and again after a role change into a production team.

## Vendors

A vendor that will handle customer data needs a signed data-processing agreement before any access is granted. The security team records the approval in the vendor register.
